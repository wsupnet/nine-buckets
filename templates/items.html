Move value between two manually-valued items — bank → brokerage cash, cash → a manual asset, or cash → a liability to pay it down. Net worth is unchanged; only where the money sits changes. To move units of a stock, coin or metal between two places, use Move holdings. To record a purchase, add a lot on the Holdings page — that moves the cash for you.

Move units of a holding from one place to another — cold wallet → exchange, broker → broker, safe → vault. This is not a sale: the quantity and its cost basis both move, so nothing is realized and the units keep their original purchase dates for FIFO. Only the transfer fee reduces net worth. Both sides must be the same ticker.
